Quim Motger

Assistant Professor · ESSI · GESSI · UPC-BarcelonaTech

Requirements Engineering LLMs & NLP Agentic Systems User Feedback & App Review Mining

I am an Assistant Professor (professor lector) at the Department of Service and Information System Engineering (ESSI) of the Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC-BarcelonaTech), and a member of the Software Engineering and AI-based Systems Research Group (GESSI). My research spans requirements engineering, large language models, agentic systems and user feedback analysis, and has been published in journals such as IEEE TSE, ACM Computing Surveys and Empirical Software Engineering, and in conferences such as RE, REFSQ and SANER. I take part in European and national research projects, including HIVEMIND (Horizon Europe) and MLEvol, and I supervise PhD students in UPC's Computing doctoral programme. Since 2018, I have taught Bachelor's and Master's courses at UPC and at the Universitat Oberta de Catalunya (UOC) on web applications, databases, requirements engineering and object-oriented design.

I hold a PhD in Computing from UPC (2024, cum laude, International Doctorate Mention), including a research stay at the Istituto di Linguistica Computazionale "Antonio Zampolli" (CNR, Pisa). My thesis received the SISTEDES Best Doctoral Thesis Award 2026 (ex aequo). I am active in the requirements engineering community: I sit on the Steering Committee of the AIRE workshop and serve as Publicity Chair of the Requirements Engineering Journal. I have also held several organising roles at RE, REFSQ and RCIS, serve on the programme committees of RE and REFSQ, and regularly review for journals such as ACM TOSEM, JSS and IST.
